# Support

- **Bugs and questions:** open an issue on GitHub (Issues tab of this repository). Say which Frappe version,
  browser, operating system and token brand you use, and attach `connector.log` from the signer's computer with
  the PIN never included (it never is).
- **E-mail:** the support address shown on the Frappe Cloud Marketplace page.
- **Before writing:** the Troubleshooting tables in `README.md` and `connector/README.md` cover the usual
  messages (connector not running, driver not found, token not found, wrong PIN, expired certificate).

Please do not send PINs, passwords or private keys. Nobody needs them to help you.

connector on your signers' computers, the signature box on your own print format, a first signed invoice
with your real token, and a walkthrough for your team — a one-time remote setup is **USD 250** (pay the INR
equivalent by UPI). Write to the support e-mail with your site name and preferred time.
